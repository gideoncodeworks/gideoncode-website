We’ll cancel your paid subscription and flip your account to sponsored the day you sign on for the website. You stop getting billed by Modern Drive that same day.

Not a promo. The comp is standard for every web client and it doesn’t expire. Your rate is locked when you sign on — it goes up only if you materially change scope (add more sites, e-commerce, etc.).
