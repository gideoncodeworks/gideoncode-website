Cleveland Web Design FAQ

Do you build websites for Cleveland businesses? +

Yes. Gideon Codeworks builds websites for small businesses across Cleveland and the surrounding area. We handle design, copywriting, hosting, and ongoing updates — so business owners don’t have to deal with tech.

How much does a website cost for a Cleveland business? +

Monthly plans start at $212.50/mo + $497 setup on a 24-month contract (Starter tier). Growth is $252.50/mo, Domination is $299/mo, and Enterprise runs $699/mo. One-time builds are $2,497 to $4,997 if you’d rather own it outright. Full pricing on services.

What is included in Gideon Codeworks service? +

Every tier includes custom design, professional copywriting, hosting, SSL, ongoing content updates, SEO, mobile responsiveness, and unlimited technical support. Higher tiers add more pages, e-commerce, and Google Ads management.

How long does it take to launch a new website? +

Most sites launch within 2–4 weeks of signing. Timing depends on how quickly you can get content and photos to us — we handle everything else.

Do you offer ongoing support after launch? +

Yes — that is the whole model. Every tier includes unlimited content updates, hosting, security, and technical support for as long as you are a client. No hourly fees.
