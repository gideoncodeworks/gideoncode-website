How it works

How custom software projects go.

  1. 1

    Discovery call (45–60 min)

    What your business actually does, what breaks today, what you've tried, what success looks like. No slide deck, no sales pitch.

  2. 2

    Scoped proposal with fixed project price

    Phases, deliverables, timeline, and a fixed-price number — no hourly meter running. Changes to scope mean a conversation, not a surprise invoice.

  3. 3

    Weekly working previews

    Every week you get a working preview URL. Click around, give feedback, see progress. No "we'll show you at the end" surprise reveal.

  4. 4

    Launch + ongoing support

    Site goes live with monitoring, backups, and a documented handoff. Ongoing maintenance / feature work is quoted monthly or per project — your call.
